# Materials-1

Executive Summary
This is a senior quantitative and governance-focused role within E.ON's energy trading business.
The position combines quantitative modelling, forward curve construction, model validation,
market analysis, governance, stakeholder management, and strategic decision support.

The successful candidate will be responsible for defining and maintaining methodologies used
to construct forward curves across the organization, ensuring they are robust, transparent,
validated, documented, and suitable for business use.

What Forward Curve Modelling Means
Forward curves represent expected future prices for energy products such as power and gas.
Because markets do not trade every future delivery period, quantitative methodologies are used
to transform observable market prices into complete curves that can be used for valuation,
risk management, forecasting, and trading decisions.

This role focuses on owning and improving those methodologies.

Key Responsibility: Quantitative Ownership & Methodological Leadership
• Design, evolve and maintain group-wide forward curve construction methodologies.
• Define standards, principles and best practices used throughout the organization.
• Review assumptions, parameter choices and expert judgement.
• Challenge methodologies where necessary.
• Lead model validation activities including back-testing, sensitivity analysis and model reviews.
• Assess model limitations and determine suitability for business use.
• Drive continuous improvement of quantitative frameworks.

Key Responsibility: Market Analysis & Curve Quality
• Monitor market developments and liquidity conditions.
• Evaluate structural changes in energy markets.
• Assess implications of market developments on forward curve construction.
• Define curve quality metrics and monitoring frameworks.
• Ensure consistency, reliability and robustness of pricing curves.
• Improve quantitative controls and quality assurance processes.

Key Responsibility: Governance & Stakeholder Management
• Act as the central interface between curve owners, quantitative analysts,
trading desks, risk management teams and senior management.
• Coordinate quantitative and technical development activities.
• Maintain documentation, policies, standards and governance procedures.
• Organize curve approval and update cycles.
• Ensure methodologies are properly reviewed and approved before implementation.

Technical Requirements
• Master's degree or PhD in Finance, Mathematics, Physics, Engineering,
Economics or a related field.
• 5–7+ years of experience in quantitative modelling, forward curve construction,
trading analytics or risk management within energy markets.
• Strong knowledge of statistics, stochastic modelling and time-series analysis.
• Advanced Python programming skills.
• Experience working with databases, data platforms and cloud technologies.
• Ability to review and understand complex model code.

Business & Communication Requirements
• Strong communication and presentation skills.
• Ability to explain complex quantitative concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
• Confidence presenting findings to senior management.
• Capability to challenge assumptions constructively.
• Independent working style and strong stakeholder management skills.

Ideal Candidate Profile
The ideal candidate is an experienced quantitative professional with deep knowledge
of energy markets, forward curve construction and model governance. They combine strong
technical expertise with business understanding and can influence decisions across trading,
risk and management functions.

Career Level Assessment
This role appears to be above a standard Senior Quantitative Analyst position.
The emphasis on methodology ownership, governance, model validation, stakeholder management
and organizational standards suggests a Quant Lead, Methodology Lead or Senior Specialist level role.
